Welcome to MatrimonyConnect ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our matrimony services. By using our services, you agree to the terms described below.
Section 01

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you register or use our platform:

  • Personal Information: Name, date of birth, gender, religion, caste, community, mother tongue, and marital status.
  • Contact Information: Email address, mobile number, and postal address.
  • Profile Information: Education, occupation, income, family details, horoscope/kundali data, photos, and partner preferences.
  • Payment Information: When you purchase a paid plan, we collect transaction details through our payment partners (Razorpay / Cashfree). We do not store your card details on our servers.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies when you access our site.
Section 02

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your matrimony profile.
  • To suggest compatible matches based on your preferences and community.
  • To send you notifications, matches, and service updates via email or SMS.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription plan.
  • To improve our platform, features, and customer support experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or misuse.
Section 03

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited situations:

  • With Other Members: Your profile (as configured by you) is visible to other registered members for matchmaking purposes.
  • With Payment Partners: We share necessary transaction data with Razorpay and Cashfree to process your payments securely.
  • With Service Providers: We may share data with trusted vendors (SMS gateways, email services) who help run our platform.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or a government authority.
Section 04

Data Security

🔐

Your data is protected with industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, secure servers, and strict role-based access controls. We regularly audit our security practices.

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security against all threats.

Section 05

Cookies

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your login and preferences, and analyse site usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of our website may not function correctly without them.

Section 06

Your Rights

  • You may access, correct, or update your profile information at any time from your account settings.
  • You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at support@matrimonyconnect.in.
  • You may opt out of all promotional communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
Section 07

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and strongly encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it immediately.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
