Who We Are

India's Most Trusted Matrimony Platform

MatrimonyConnect was founded with a single heartfelt mission — to help every Indian find a loving, compatible life partner. We understand the sanctity of marriage in our culture and built a platform that honours your traditions while embracing modern technology.

Our platform serves members from all communities, castes, and religions across India and the global Indian diaspora. Safe, verified, and deeply personal — we make the journey to forever a joyful one.

Our Journey

A Decade of Bringing Hearts Together

2015 — The Beginning

MatrimonyConnect was born from a simple belief: every person deserves a loving and compatible partner. We started by serving local Tamil Nadu communities.

2018 — Going National

Expanded our reach across all Indian states, adding 50+ community-specific matchmaking filters and horoscope compatibility tools.

2021 — Technology Upgrade

Launched AI-powered match recommendations, verified profile badges, and a mobile-first experience loved by over 2 lakh active users.

2025 — Trusted by 5 Lakh+ Families

Today, MatrimonyConnect stands as one of India's most trusted platforms, with over 50,000 successful marriages and growing every single day.
